              📚 How To Play (Live Game)
              1. ✍️Type your answer as COORDINATE + NUMBER, e.g. A5 7
              2. ↪A5 7 puts a 7 in Row A, Column 5
              3. 📁Rows are letters A-I (top to bottom)
              4. 📂Columns are numbers 1-9 (left to right)
              5. ❌No punctuation needed - just a coordinate, a space, and a number
              6. ⭐Each correct guess earns 1 point
              7. ⏰No time limit - the clock just shows how long each puzzle takes
              🧠 New To Sudoku? Rules Of The Puzzle
              1. 📈The board is a 9x9 grid split into nine 3x3 boxes
              2. 🎯Goal: fill every empty cell with a digit from 1 to 9
              3. ↔️Each row must contain 1-9 with no repeats
              4. ↕️Each column must contain 1-9 with no repeats
              5. 🔹Each bold-bordered 3x3 box must also contain 1-9 with no repeats
              6. 🔒Some numbers are already filled in ("givens") and can't be changed - use them as clues
              7. 💡Tip: scan a row, column or box for the digit it's missing, then find the only cell it can legally go in
              8. ✅The puzzle is solved once every cell is filled and all three rules hold everywhere
